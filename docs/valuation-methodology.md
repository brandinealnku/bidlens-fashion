# Valuation methodology

Eligible included, same-currency, category-matched comparables require a score of 50 and positive price. Duplicate identifiers are removed. Weight is similarity × quality × status × recency × conservative condition adjustment. Status weights are 1.0 verified sold, .85 user-reported sold, .35 active, .20 estimated, and .10 unknown. Weighted quartiles produce quick, expected, and optimistic estimates; IQR flags outliers. Fewer than three records caps confidence and forces limited-evidence review. Active asks are not sold evidence.

Financial calculations use cents and basis points. The maximum all-in cost is the lesser of net proceeds minus required profit and net proceeds divided by one plus required ROI. Binary search solves the highest hammer whose exact premium, taxable components, tax, and fixed costs fit, then rounds down to the bid increment.
